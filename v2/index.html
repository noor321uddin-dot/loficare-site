Yes. Referrer earnings on every bill, barcoded samples from collection to delivery, verification before release, and fast report delivery are the core of the diagnostics tier.

A chamber tool runs one desk. LofiCare runs the whole facility on one record, and the appointment module is the first step of it, not a separate app.
